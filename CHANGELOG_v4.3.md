# v4.3.0 — ZERO FRONT MULTI-PATH FINALE & CAMPAIGN ENDGAME

## Major milestone

v4.3 turns the campaign approach generated by v4.2 into a materially different endgame across rounds 91–100. The new layer does not replace the existing Zero Front finale, boss legends, round progression, health, economy or projectile authority. It composes with them.

## Three campaign-derived Zero Front doctrines

### IRON SHIELD
- Defensive endgame built around preserving Orzelek integrity.
- Rounds 91–99 use hold-the-line kill objectives with an Orzelek HP floor.
- Repeated combat chains repair Orzelek and can repair the player.
- Periodic armor/module servicing is granted at major staging rounds.
- Final entry package heavily repairs and shields both the player and Orzelek.
- Heavy/Siege enemies receive a modest durability response so the doctrine remains a real defensive test.

### BREAKTHROUGH
- Offensive endgame focused on breaking armored formations.
- Rounds 91–99 require Heavy/Siege/Boss pressure kills, with fallback progress from sustained combat so objectives cannot deadlock on an unusual spawn composition.
- Armored kills feed AP/Explosive logistics and late Plasma resupply.
- Heavy/Siege/Boss enemies gain stronger durability to create a genuine breakthrough challenge.
- Final entry package is a high-output AP/Explosive/Plasma reserve.

### GHOST SPEAR
- Recon/intelligence endgame focused on Elite/Sniper/Supply/Boss priority targets.
- Rounds 91–99 use priority-hunt objectives with fallback progress from sustained combat.
- Priority kills feed EMP ammunition and War Bonds.
- Elite/Sniper/Supply enemies gain selective durability rather than a global stat increase.
- Final entry package emphasizes EMP/AP control and strategic intelligence rewards.

## Doctrine objectives and mastery
- Every round from 91 to 99 receives a doctrine-specific optional combat objective.
- Objectives are tracked from real EnemyTank deaths and existing enemy classifications.
- Completed objectives award War Bonds through WarEconomyDirector and doctrine-specific supplies/repairs.
- Iron Shield objectives can be compromised if Orzelek falls below the doctrine integrity floor.
- Completing at least 7 of the 9 doctrine objectives grants Zero Front Doctrine Mastery and an additional final reserve.
- Mastery and missed-objective state are persisted per campaign run.

## Integrated endgame pressure
- New enemy pressure uses Health.SetMaximum on already spawned enemies; no second health model or alternate spawn authority is introduced.
- Reinforcement is class-selective and doctrine-specific.
- Existing v4.0 ZeroFrontFinaleDirector remains active for baseline phase progression, emergency support and final completion reward.
- v4.3 adds strategic differentiation on top of that baseline instead of duplicating it.

## HUD / UX
- New Zero Front Doctrine panel appears during rounds 91–100.
- Shows active doctrine, round objective, progress, status, kill counts and doctrine mastery progress.
- Large banners communicate objective activation, completion, compromise and final assault state.

## Compatibility / architecture
- Uses CampaignEventDirector.CurrentApproach from v4.2 as the doctrine source.
- Uses TankGame, CombatRoster, EnemyTank, Health, PlayerTank, ArmorSystem, WarEconomyDirector, BattleAudio and VisualFactory.
- No new currency, health pool, round manager, enemy registry or projectile system.
- Boss Legends, Tactical Terrain, Dynamic Frontline, Friendly Support and the existing Zero Front finale remain authoritative and active.
