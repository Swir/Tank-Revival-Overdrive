# Tank Revival: Orzeł Overdrive v0.2.0

This release turns the playable prototype into the first full Eagle-defense gameplay milestone.

### Highlights

- Defend the new Orzełek stronghold across 100 rounds.
- Hunt colored Supply Tanks to obtain six finite special ammunition classes plus unlimited Standard shells.
- AP penetration, HE area damage, incendiary burn, EMP disable, Twin Shot and Plasma penetration.
- Player upgrades and special ammunition persist between rounds and respawns.
- New Siege and Elite enemy roles plus dedicated Supply units.
- Boss every 10 rounds with ten escalating special-fire tiers; round 100 adds multi-stage radial barrages.
- Procedural runtime audio for engines, cannons, heavy weapons, explosions, ricochets, alarms, pickups, EMP and plasma.
- New 2.5D procedural tank construction, animated track segments, muzzle flashes, projectile trails, damage smoke, sparks, track marks and battlefield debris.
- More aggressive round-by-round scaling and four enemy spawn lanes.
- Windows x64 portable build; Unity is not required to play.
