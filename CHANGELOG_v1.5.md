# Tank Revival: Orzeł Overdrive — v1.5 ENEMY FACTIONS & WAR COMMANDERS

## Major gameplay systems

- Five campaign factions now rotate across the 100-round war: Iron Legion, Scorch Brigade, Storm Corps, Black Guard and Overdrive Host.
- Every enemy tank receives a live tactical battlefield role instead of being differentiated only by base class stats.
- Breachers prioritize Orzełek and launch dedicated demolition fire.
- Raiders perform burst attacks against the player and gain EMP behavior in Storm Corps sectors.
- Suppressors launch spread volleys and begin pressuring Orzełek directly in the second half of the campaign.
- Engineers repair nearby enemy formations.
- Black Guard units receive timed defensive shielding; Overdrive Host units operate at accelerated doctrine tempo.

## War Commanders

- Dedicated commander assaults begin from round 15 and intensify in the late campaign.
- Commanders are promoted from actual Heavy/Siege/Elite formations rather than spawning as disconnected bonus targets.
- Commanders gain extra durability, visible command rings and faction identity.
- Command pulses repair and temporarily shield nearby enemy armor.
- Coordinated commander volleys increasingly prioritize Orzełek as the campaign escalates.
- Commander ammunition changes by faction, including Incendiary, EMP and late-game AP/Plasma pressure.
- Killing a commander repairs Orzełek, repairs the player's tank, grants special ammunition and records persistent commander defeats.

## Campaign readability

- New faction/doctrine HUD identifies the current enemy army and strategy.
- Active commanders are clearly announced in the HUD and visually marked on the battlefield.
- Role badges communicate Breacher, Raider, Suppressor, Engineer and Vanguard behavior.

## Design rule

Orzełek remains the strategic heart of Tank Revival. Environmental effects do not destroy the core directly, while organized enemy formations, Breachers, Siege units, bosses and War Commanders are explicitly designed to break through and destroy it.
