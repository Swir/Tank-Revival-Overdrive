# v2.0.0 — TOTAL OVERDRIVE

Major campaign-structure milestone built on validated v1.9 Destruction & Warzone Reforged.

## Campaign acts
- 100 rounds are grouped into five 20-round acts with distinct campaign banners and progression tracking.
- Entering a new act delivers an integrated field resupply using existing repair and ammunition systems.
- Sustained combat performance fills an Overdrive Charge meter; reaching 100% triggers a real combat surge with shield, special ammo and periodic Orzelek recovery.

## Strategic directives
- Every fifth non-boss round can carry an optional strategic directive.
- Directives include protecting Orzelek without HP loss, purging priority armor, or preserving tank armor.
- Successful directives reward repair, special ammunition and Orzelek recovery and are tracked persistently.

## Final round 100
- OVERDRIVE PRIME receives a dedicated final protocol layered on top of Boss Legends and existing boss phases.
- The final boss gains additional core armor, three health-gated protocol escalations, radial barrages, targeted anti-armor bursts and increasing pressure on both the player and Orzelek.
- Round 100 presents a dedicated final-battle HUD and preserves the existing rule that victory requires clearing the battlefield while Orzelek survives.

## Stability
- No existing combat authority is replaced: TankGame, Boss Legends, ArmorSystem, factions, Eagle Siege, fortress, engineering, ammunition and destruction systems remain authoritative.
- Stable main is not updated until Windows x64 CI succeeds and the milestone is playtest-ready.
